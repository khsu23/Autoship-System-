# Autoship Discount Rules
Version: 1.0

## First Autoship order

Eligible pet parents receive 35% off their first Autoship order, up to a maximum discount of $20.00.

**Exception:** A customer who has previously redeemed the first-order offer is not eligible again, even if they cancel and later re-subscribe.

## Recurring Autoship orders

Eligible Autoship renewals receive 5% off the item price.

**Exception:** Promotions do not stack. If another eligible promotion has higher priority, the higher-priority promotion applies instead.
