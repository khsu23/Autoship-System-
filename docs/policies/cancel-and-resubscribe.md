# Cancel and Re-subscribe Rules
Version: 1.0

## Canceling Autoship

Pet parents may cancel an active or paused Autoship before the next shipment processes. Cancellation stops future shipments.

**Exception:** An order already processing cannot be canceled through Autoship management and needs support assistance.

## Re-subscribing

A canceled Autoship can be re-subscribed with the current product, cadence, and shipping address.

**Exception:** Re-subscribing does not restore the first-order Autoship discount. The recurring 5% discount is used when eligible.
